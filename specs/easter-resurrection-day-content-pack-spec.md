# Easter / Resurrection Day Content Pack Spec

Shared behavior (category visibility, season calendar, pack format, predefined events, featured seasonal event, Daily Challenge integration, authoring standards, admin, testing) is defined in `themed-content-spec.md`. This spec covers only Easter / Resurrection Day content.

## Purpose

The Easter / Resurrection Day pack adds seasonal rounds focused on Jesus' death, burial, and resurrection, Passion Week, resurrection witnesses, Gospel chronology, and Old Testament prophecy and foreshadowing.

The pack uses "Resurrection Day" language where appropriate while staying discoverable for users who think in terms of Easter.

Lent, Palm Sunday, and Holy Week are part of this pack, handled with session tags. Pentecost has its own pack (`pentecost-content-pack-spec.md`); Passover is covered in depth by `biblical-feasts-content-pack-spec.md`.

## Target Users

- Families during Holy Week and Easter / Resurrection Day gatherings
- Churches, youth groups, and Sunday school classes
- Teachers building Passion Week lesson openers
- Event hosts running a Resurrection-themed night

## Content Themes

Primary themes:

- Jesus' road to Jerusalem and his predictions of his death (Lent)
- Passion Week chronology
- The Last Supper and its Passover setting
- Gethsemane
- Jesus' trials
- Crucifixion
- Burial
- Empty tomb
- Resurrection appearances
- Witnesses and responses
- Old Testament prophecy and foreshadowing
- Victory, hope, forgiveness, and new life

Avoid:

- Generic spring trivia (eggs, bunnies)
- Details from tradition not grounded in the text (for example the Stations of the Cross, Veronica)
- Flattening differences between Gospel accounts

## Tradition Versus Text and Parallel Accounts

The four Gospels tell the Passion and Resurrection with different details. Per `themed-content-spec.md` 10.3, prompts about these details **name the Gospel**.

Examples with study notes:

- At the tomb: Matthew describes an angel (28:2-5); Mark a young man (16:5); Luke two men (24:4); John two angels (20:12).
- Simon of Cyrene carried the cross (Matthew 27:32, Mark 15:21, Luke 23:26); John describes Jesus bearing his cross (John 19:17).
- Palm branches are named in John 12:13; the other Gospels say branches or garments.
- Matthew mentions an ass and a colt (21:2-7); Mark, Luke, and John mention a colt or young ass.
- The two men crucified with Jesus are not named.
- The full seating at the Last Supper is not described; John mentions only the disciple leaning on Jesus' bosom (John 13:23-25).

Tradition-versus-text rounds follow the same rule: state what the text says, cite it, and do not use interpretive claims as the lie.

## Content Ideas

### Passion Week Chronology

Candidate events: triumphal entry, cleansing the temple, teaching in the temple, Judas agreeing to betray Jesus, Last Supper, Gethsemane, arrest, Peter's denial, trials before the council, Pilate, and Herod, crucifixion, darkness, temple veil torn, burial, women visiting the tomb, road to Emmaus, Thomas seeing the risen Jesus, Great Commission.

### Who Said It?

Candidate speakers: Jesus, Peter, Judas, Pilate, Pilate's wife, the crowd, the thief on the cross, the centurion, Mary Magdalene, the angels at the tomb, Thomas, Cleopas.

### Prophecy and Fulfillment

Candidate passages and themes:

- Psalm 16, 22, 69
- Isaiah 50, 52-53
- Zechariah 9, 12
- Jonah as a sign (Matthew 12:40)
- Genesis 3
- Exodus and Passover themes (1 Corinthians 5:7)
- Matthew 26-28; Mark 14-16; Luke 22-24; John 18-21
- 1 Corinthians 15

### Bible Connections

Example groups:

- Garden scenes: Eden, Gethsemane, the tomb garden, the resurrection appearance
- Trial voices: chief priests, Pilate, Herod, the crowd
- Cross witnesses: Mary, John, soldiers, centurion
- Resurrection witnesses: Mary Magdalene, Peter, Thomas, the Emmaus disciples
- Fulfillment themes: betrayed, pierced, numbered, risen

### Odd One Out

- People present near the cross vs one not present in the passage
- Resurrection appearance witnesses vs one Passion Week-only figure
- Places in Passion Week vs one unrelated Gospel location
- Prophecy references tied to suffering vs one unrelated wisdom passage

### Bible Anagrams

Examples: RISEN, TOMB, GOLGOTHA, GETHSEMANE, EMMAUS, HOSANNA, PILATE, THOMAS.

### Verse Scramble and Complete the Verse

Candidate verses: John 11:25, John 3:16, Matthew 28:6, Luke 24:6, 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, 1 Corinthians 15:55, Isaiah 53:5, Romans 6:4.

| `resurrection` | Resurrection Day to Easter + 7 | Empty tomb and appearances |

### Holy Week Day-by-Day

`holy-week` sessions carry `sequenceDay`:

| Day | Date | Focus |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Palm Sunday | Triumphal entry |
| 2 | Monday | Cleansing the temple |
| 3 | Tuesday | Teaching and controversies in the temple |
| 4 | Wednesday | Judas agrees to betray Jesus |
| 5 | Thursday | Last Supper, Gethsemane, arrest |
| 6 | Friday | Trials, crucifixion, burial |
| 7 | Saturday | The tomb is sealed and guarded |
| 8 | Resurrection Day | Empty tomb and first appearances |

The day assignments for Monday through Wednesday follow common harmonies; prompts avoid stating the day of the week as fact where the text does not.

`sequenceDay` only chooses which day's rounds are in the pool. Within a day, rounds play in random order like all other content (`themed-content-spec.md` section 20).

- Meets `themed-content-spec.md`.
- Round targets met for every included game.

- The `resurrection-kids` event plays entirely on easy content.
