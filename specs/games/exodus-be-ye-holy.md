# Be Ye Holy — Exodus Journey checkpoint and standalone game
Status: **design approved in conversation 2026-10-16; biblical verse-level audit and authored scenarios pending**. Parent Journey #461; composition #458/#460. Scope: Leviticus 11–15, 19–20. Do not confuse this material with the book-of-Exodus-only boundary; the wider wilderness continuation must be labeled appropriately.

## Experience and presentation
One living wilderness Israelite encampment, shared sequential encounters for the entire congregation. Main Stage camera moves from aerial camp view into an encounter and back, optionally orbiting; illustrated visuals baseline, selective animation optional, essential real-time movement when required. Ambient camp activity optional if affordable. Dialogue, instructions, Scripture and narration **always appear as text**, regardless of optional future voice audio. Automatic camera/dialogue sequencing, pausing at player decisions; Host does not manually advance every line. Stable authored IDs for assets and optional audio. No competitive scores, public mistake attribution, holiness meter or invented divine punishment.

## Participation and roles
Players select one role for the checkpoint: Farmer, Merchant, Shepherd, Household Caretaker, Community Helper, Craftsperson, Camp Steward. Roles are not exclusive, and the catalog may expand without engine changes. Every role available at every age; choices use individual profile/age-appropriate wording and complexity. Roles are independent of team membership; multiple players may share a role; unclaimed roles receive authored NPC substitutions. Priests and prescribed authorities are NPCs. Roles are narrative perspectives, not exclusive topic jurisdictions. Multiple roles may participate sequentially in a scenario; role ownership remains stable during that scenario.

The director balances opportunities across roles and players, favoring those with fewer meaningful **decisions**, not mere appearances. Within a role use round-robin. If an assigned player becomes unavailable before their first decision, move them back exactly one place in that role's queue (e.g. 2→5→8 becomes 5→2→8). After a committed decision, NPC takeover preserves it. No prior encounter outcomes persist into later Be Ye Holy encounters.

Recurring named NPCs have stable personalities; one-off NPCs are supported. Portray children, widows, strangers, the poor, elders, hired workers, persons with disabilities, parents and judges as multidimensional individuals; categories may overlap. NPCs can make authored mistakes. A widow's specific protection in Exodus 22 must not be misattributed to Leviticus 19.

## Player agency and conversation quality
**Never preselect any gameplay answer** (dialogue, classification, sequence or other decision). Player character cannot speak an opinion, propose an action or take a position before the player chooses; NPCs may neutrally invite their input. After commitment, the character performs/speaks the exact selected authored action, and subsequent dialogue must reflect it. No next line may presuppose a choice that has not occurred or contradict a prior choice. Conversation should arise naturally from camp life, with humor, concerns, hopes and genuine disagreement, not classroom prompts disguised as NPC speech. Scripture generally appears after choices in the review, not before in a way that gives away answers.

## Encounter types and coverage
All applicable commands, prohibitions, purity requirements and principles in Lev 11–15, 19–20 must be covered **across the authored library**, not in each session. One encounter may cover multiple verses and a verse may occur in multiple encounters. Include dietary distinctions and contaminated vessels/food/water/seeds; childbirth; skin conditions, fabrics and affected houses as future-land discussion; inspection, reinspection, isolation, cleansing and restoration; bodily discharges and contact impurity; gleaning, honest measures, wages, truth, fair judgment, treatment of strangers, elders and vulnerable persons; worship restrictions and serious offenses. This list is a coverage planning aid, **not** a completed verse-level audit. Authoring tool must report command/principle-to-encounter traceability and gaps. Clearly distinguish ritual impurity from moral sin and contagion. Serious penalties may be discussed but never graphically enacted. Laws presupposing possession of Canaan are discussed/planned in the wilderness, not depicted as already fulfilled.

Encounters may contain natural dialogue **and** inspections, classification, ordering, procedural steps, identification, role-specific responses and other interaction nodes within the same authored branching graph. Priestly inspections and ritual determinations remain with NPC priests; players can investigate, prepare, identify requirements, and respond. No single interaction format is mandatory.

## Scenario Director
Seeded, deterministic, authored scenario/variation selection. Eligible content is filtered by age preset (Children, Youth, Teen, Adult, All Ages), individual topic toggles and the **youngest participating player's eligibility** in shared mixed-age sessions. Host overrides may narrow but never bypass youngest eligibility. Content filters do not erase comprehensive library coverage. No Host pregame coverage report; coverage matrix is authoring-tool only.

Normal selection balances biblical topic coverage and meaningful player participation **equally**, mixes simple and complex encounters throughout, honors scenario role requirements and narrative suitability, and favors under-participating players. Prefer unseen scenarios across sessions, then previously encountered scenarios with different **substantive authored variations**; no exact scenario repetition within a session and no cosmetic name-only reskins. Support both stand-alone scenarios and scenario families. Long-term library target 150–200 distinct authored encounters, not a claim that these already exist.

**Duration must never affect normal scenario choice.** Only when approaching a configured checkpoint ending target may the director favor shorter eligible encounters using calibrated effort estimates. No configured ending target means duration is irrelevant to selection. Host may pause/resume, skip, or select next encounter. Host chooses mixed/topic-focused session and duration preset/custom scheduling.

## Decisions and pacing
Optional anonymous observer judgments before the active player's commitment; close on commitment and never hold the decision waiting for observers. Active player may Seek Counsel up to twice per checkpoint; instantly display a snapshot of received observer judgments; no responses means no use consumed. Soft timer pauses briefly within authored cap; counsel is not a correctness oracle. Complexity-dependent soft decision reminders, illustrative standard 30s first, 45s second, 60s authored NPC takeover, tunable and non-punitive.

## Scripture review
**Every completed scenario**, regardless of decisions, immediately presents branch-aware Scripture review that addresses the actual topics and choices. Show relevant verses; detailed mode may show surrounding context. Host selects brief/detailed review and may enable optional reflection/discussion after review. Explain the biblical command and purpose when the passage supports it. No public shame or mistake scores. Review may be assembled from authored reusable components selected by visited decisions/topics, never generated by AI.

## Duration and stopping
Use Agon-wide relative effort units for actual played path, including review; no fixed authored minutes. Live estimated seconds/unit use a configurable preset baseline plus weighted observed encounter durations. Session supports a duration target (active checkpoint time, excluding Host pauses) or Host-local clock-time target (fixed despite pause unless Host changes it). When nearing target, director may use estimated durations for selection; at target, finish active scenario and its review, then do not start another. If target reached between scenarios, stop. Do not truncate branches/reviews. See [effort/duration specification](../../docs/architecture/agon-effort-duration-estimation.md) (actual path and calibration details).

## Launch modes and data
Same authoritative gameplay implementation runs as a separately selected major standalone game or as a Journey checkpoint; different authored introductions, conclusions and return targets. Inherit Journey participants/eligible packs/configuration with declared overrides; standalone initializes independently. Both obey compatible Game/Data/Translation Pack readiness, deterministic replay and vNext runtime authority. See [dual-launch architecture](../../docs/architecture/game-launch-composition.md).
