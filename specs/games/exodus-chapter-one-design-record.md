# Chapter 1 — Return to Egypt (design-approved + capability-audited)

**Prologue — Israel in Egypt:** narrative from Jacob/Joseph through new Pharaoh, oppression, Moses' preservation and flight to Midian.

### 1. Burning Bush / Call of Moses — Ex 3:1–4:17
**Classification: NEW_REUSABLE_CAPABILITY consumer -> #579 Composite Cooperative Challenge**, composed with existing Challenge Families.
- narrative scenes + cooperative discovery;
- profile-appropriate distributed clues/pieces can be privately assigned;
- contributions must materially feed shared reconstruction/synthesis of God's identity/call, Moses' objections and God's provisions;
- players discover the canonical event; they do not choose whether Moses accepts.
Exodus owns the authored clues/content/presentation, not the distributed-cooperation engine.

### 2. Moses Returns / Gather the Elders — Ex 4:18–31
**Classification: COMPOSE_EXISTING/planned Gather the Twelve + Challenge Resolver; #579 may be used if distributed team synthesis improves the final design.**
- travel narrative; retain Ex 4:24–26 with optional Dig Deeper;
- Gather Israel's elders via Twelve Tribes Challenge concept;
- do NOT claim Scripture says exactly one elder per tribe;
- conclusion remains Aaron speaks/signs performed/people believe and worship.
Do not create an Exodus-specific gathering engine.

### 3. Before Pharaoh — Ex 5:1–5
**Classification: NEW_REUSABLE_CAPABILITY/profile -> #577 Message Assembly.**
- private fragment hands; alternating reveal; active player may rearrange revealed fragments;
- no correctness leak while fragments remain hidden;
- final revealer gets first full attempt, then alternating attempts until solved;
- difficulty = fragmentation granularity Easy phrase chunks -> Expert individual words;
- DECODE remains separate;
- canonical message is delivered and Pharaoh refuses.
Exodus supplies message/content/presentation only.

### 4. Bricks Without Straw — Ex 5:6–21
**Classification: NEW_REUSABLE_CAPABILITY under validation -> #578 Cooperative Work.**
- shared Gather -> Make -> Haul -> Build pipeline;
- dynamic allocation, no permanent specialization;
- simultaneous private Challenges commit to authoritative shared state;
- supplied-straw tutorial, then supply removed while quota remains;
- fair work cycles prevent raw speed advantage;
- profile-appropriate Challenges have equivalent work consequence;
- canonical quota remains unmet.
Do not generalize beyond #578 until proving consumers justify it.

### 5. Moses Cries Out / God's Promise Renewed — Ex 5:22–6:13
**Classification: COMPOSE_EXISTING Challenge Families/Resolver; optionally #579 for distributed discovery. No new engine currently justified.**
- Phase 1: clues discover translation-aware action/promise pieces from Ex 6:6–8;
- Phase 2: cooperatively ASSEMBLE/ORDER the discovered pieces;
- present/read Scripture with discoveries highlighted;
- preserve promises in Journey Journal as remembered Scripture, not consumable powers.

### 6. Moses & Aaron Before Pharaoh / Aaron's Staff — Ex 7:1–13
**Classification: COMPOSE_EXISTING RECALL/IDENTIFY + narrative presentation.**
- optional short recall/identify setup;
- canonical cinematic sign, magicians, Aaron's staff swallows theirs, Pharaoh remains hardened;
- no new capability; not every event requires gameplay.

# Chapter 2 — Let My People Go (approved narrative direction; mechanics still being designed)
Covers plagues 1–9 and escalating Pharaoh interactions. Tenth plague belongs with Passover/Deliverance in Chapter 3. Avoid nine copies of trivia -> plague animation.

Approved design direction:
- plague/event theme may request **Bible-wide thematic Scripture Challenges**, not only facts from the current Exodus passage;
- examples include blood/water/river, frogs, dust/insects, swarms, livestock, sores/healing, hail/storm, locusts, darkness/light;
- strong Bible-knowledge profiles should receive genuinely deeper cross-Scripture recognition/relationship work rather than merely more obscure random trivia;
- mixed-profile teams should use #579 where appropriate so profile-appropriate contributions become necessary to shared cooperative synthesis;
- semantic association must distinguish "these passages share a topic" from authored claims of theological/prophetic relationship.

Potential persistent chapter presentation: record warning/sign/judgment/Pharaoh response patterns without making them a player-controlled resistance meter.
